Privacy

Passaggio does all its work on your phone, so nothing needs to be sent anywhere.

The app

  • No account, no sign-in and no cloud.
  • The app has no internet access. It asks only for the microphone, and listens only on screens that need it.
  • All voice analysis, song separation and lyric recognition run on the phone.
  • Your voice setup, results, songs and settings are stored encrypted on the phone. "Reset progress" in Settings deletes them.
  • Recordings are kept only if you turn on Save my singing to improve the trainer, and then only on your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

This website

  • No cookies and no audio recording.
  • The Download page asks GitHub for the latest file's name and size. That request follows GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • The web host keeps normal server logs (IP address and pages visited).
