Feel your technique

Hold a note and six live guides read your sound. Each turns green when it's right.

  1. 01

    Six live guides

    • Soft palate, throat space, free or pressed tone, breath, vowel shape and the bridge.
    • Each one compares you with your own voice, not an average singer.
  2. 02

    72 seconds of guided practice

    Six short steps. Each gives you a vowel to sing and one thing to try, and shows if it's working.

    Keep the feeling

    Save the sound when it's right. Later lessons remind you of it.

What Passaggio does

Most apps only check if you hit the note. Passaggio also hears how you make the sound and compares it with your own voice.

  1. 01
    While you sing

    Live pitch and technique

    • Your voice draws a line through the note bars.
    • One word tells you how you sound: clear, airy, tight or nasal.
  2. 02
    After each lesson

    Ai Warm-up: finding your voice's path

    • Works out why notes went wrong: strain, the bridge, breath or a vowel.
    • Builds your next warm-up to fix it, then checks again.
    • Always the first warm-up option.
  3. 03
    Over time

    Passaggio Ai Lessons

    Lessons built from the notes you sing. The notes, jumps and vowels you keep missing get lessons of their own.
