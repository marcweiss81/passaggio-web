A voice is always becoming

Find your voice.

Passaggio is an AI singing teacher that runs entirely on your Android phone. It hears pitch, tone and technique as you sing, shows you what to fix while you're still on the note, and builds lessons around your own range and habits.

The approach

Better singing starts with better understanding.

Most apps score pitch. Passaggio listens to how you make the sound — breath, pressing, nasality, strain, register — and judges it against your own voice, not a population average.

01 / Listen

Hear what your voice is doing

A live pitch line inside scrolling note bars, a chest-to-head resonance glow and a one-word tone read-out, all at 60 fps on the phone.

02 / Understand

Corrections that name the fix

When a habit repeats, a brass card appears with the body mechanic to change — "Lift the middle of your tongue" — and the trainer plays "not like this… like this".

03 / Grow

Lessons built from your singing

Twelve skill tracks, warm-ups with a ready check, and AI lessons generated from the notes, leaps, vowels and bridge crossings you miss most.

Inside Passaggio

Everything a lesson needs, on one phone.

Your voice, measured first

Voice setup finds your speaking pitch, range, voice type and bridge, and records your own vowels so every judgement is relative to you.

Voice setup and voice map →

A trainer who sings

Ginevra or Rocco speaks each exercise, then a real singer sings the starting note in your key. Italian-accented, warm, never robotic.

Meet the trainer →

Combos and flames

Stay on pitch and the chain grows; from six in a row the bars catch fire, with a quiet word from the trainer at ten, twenty, thirty.

While you sing →

Sing your own songs

Import a track from your phone: vocals separated on-device, melody tracked, lyrics synced by listening, then sing it over the backing.

Your songs →

Six styles, one honest judge

Classical, musical theatre, pop, rock, jazz, folk. Belt, twang and breath are only flagged where your style treats them as faults.

Tracks and levels →

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no cloud, no internet permission in the release build. Every analysis runs on the device and every result stays there, encrypted.
