A voice is always becoming

Find your voice.

Passaggio is a singing teacher that lives on your Android phone. It listens while you sing, shows you what to fix while you're still on the note, and builds lessons around your own voice and your own habits.

Start here · Feel your technique

See your technique while you sing it.

Hold a note and six live guides show what your voice is doing: soft palate, space in the throat, a free or pressed sound, breath, vowel shape and the bridge. Each one turns green when it sits in its zone. When they all settle, the head-and-shoulders photo glows and you know you've found it.

01 / Live guides

Six guides, one sound

Palate, throat space, free sound, breath, vowel and bridge, each measured from your own voice against your own reference, never against an average singer.

02 / Guided practice

72 seconds, six steps

Each 12-second step names one guide, the vowel to sing and one thing to try, then tells you live whether that guide is in its zone. At the end you see which ones you held.

03 / Remember it

Keep the feeling

When everything lines up, tap Remember it. Passaggio saves that sound as your reference and brings the same feeling back in later lessons.

A lesson that stops to fix you.

Sing guided by note bars. When a bad habit creeps in, the lesson pauses and a card tells you exactly what to change in your body. Between lessons, Passaggio works out what you keep missing and builds the next lesson around it.

The approach

Better singing starts with better understanding.

Most apps only check whether you hit the note. Passaggio listens to how you make the sound — your breath, whether you're squeezing, singing through your nose, straining, or switching between chest and head voice — and compares it with your own voice, not with everyone else's.

01 / Listen

Hear what your voice is doing

A line follows your voice through the note bars as you sing, a glow shows whether you're in chest or head voice, and one plain word tells you how you sound right now: clear, airy, tight, nasal.

02 / Understand

Corrections that name the fix

When the same mistake keeps happening, a card appears with the physical change to make — "Open the back of your mouth, like the start of a yawn" — and the trainer plays you the wrong way, then the right way.

03 / Grow

Lessons built from your singing

Twelve step-by-step courses, warm-ups that check you're ready, and personal lessons built from the notes, jumps and vowels you miss most often.
