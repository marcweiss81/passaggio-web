How it works

From first note to results.

Headphones in, phone at arm's length, a quiet room. Here is what happens next.

1

Voice setup

Speak a sentence, sing a step test up and down with the trainer, hold "ah", "ee" and "oo" on a comfortable note, and slide a siren through your range. Passaggio now knows your speaking pitch, range, voice type, bridge and what your own vowels sound like.

2

Pick a style and a lesson

Declare your style, then take Today's plan, a track and level, a warm-up, a favourite, or one of your songs. Each lesson is transposed into your range before it starts.

3

The trainer sets you up

Ginevra or Rocco explains the exercise, a real singer sings the starting note in your key, the piano counts you in. From then on the guide piano strikes each note just before its bar and rings through it.

4

Sing the lane

Bars scroll into the playhead; your pitch line draws live, gold when you're on it. The HUD shows the live note, a cents dial, the chest-to-head glow and a tone word. Stay in tune and the combo grows; from six the bars burn.

5

Corrections, when earned

The first time a habit is heard it's noted silently. The second time a brass card appears with the fix; tap it to hear "not like this… like this" and see the drawing. Keep repeating it and the explanation climbs the seven-rung ladder.

6

Results

A weighted score, round by round, what was seen once, what to work on, and a "Working on" chip for any weak area the lesson fed. 80 % opens the next level.

Under the hood

An engine built to run on the phone.

Low-latency capture

Native audio capture at 48 kHz into a lock-free buffer, analysed on its own thread with 10 ms hops. Output latency is measured per headphone route so the bars line up with what you hear.

Neural pitch tracking

A CREPE-class neural tracker fused with probabilistic YIN tracking, with dedicated modes for lip trills and hums so semi-occluded sounds don't slip to the wrong octave.

Tone against your baseline

Breathy, pressed, twang, high larynx, nasal, strain, fry, belt and distortion are read against your own recorded vowels, moved along your pitch trend — never a population norm.

Register and mix

Chest-to-head balance from spectral cues plus your measured bridge gives a connected-mix reading, so a well-joined upper note reads as "connected", not "chest".

60 fps lane

Skia and Reanimated on the UI thread, driven by the audio clock. The flames are a procedural shader, not sprites.

Local, encrypted

Results live in an encrypted SQLite database with the key in the Android keystore. The release build has no internet permission at all.
