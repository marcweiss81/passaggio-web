How it works

Put on headphones, hold your phone at arm's length and find a quiet room. Then:

1

Voice setup

Speak a sentence, sing up and down with the trainer, hold "ah", "ee" and "oo", and slide through your range. Passaggio learns your range, voice type, bridge and vowel sounds.

2

Pick a style and a lesson

Choose classical, musical theatre, pop, rock, jazz or folk. Then pick the Ai Warm-up, Today's plan, a skill track or one of your songs. Every lesson fits your range.

3

The lesson starts

The trainer explains the exercise, a real singer sings your first note, and the lesson starts by itself. The piano plays each note just before you sing it.

4

Sing with the note bars

Your voice draws a line through the bars, gold when you're on pitch. You also see your note, chest or head voice, and one word for your tone.

5

Corrections

When the same habit comes back, a card shows the fix. Tap it to hear the wrong way and the right way.

6

Results and next steps

A score, round by round, and what to work on. 80% opens the next level. The Ai Warm-up uses what it heard to plan your next session.

How it listens

Instant

It checks your voice 100 times a second, so the line keeps up with you.

In time with your headphones

It measures your headphones' delay, so the bars line up with what you hear.

Compared with you

Tone checks compare you with your own recorded vowels, not an average singer.

Chest, head and the bridge

It knows where your voice changes gear, so it can tell a smooth high note from one pushed up in chest voice.

Finds the cause

After each lesson it looks for the likely reason notes went wrong, then tests it in your next warm-up.

Nothing leaves your phone

All analysis runs on the phone. The app has no internet access, so nothing is uploaded.
