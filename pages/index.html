The approach

Better singing starts with better understanding.

Most apps only check whether you hit the note. Passaggio listens to how you make the sound — your breath, whether you're squeezing, singing through your nose, straining, or switching between chest and head voice — and compares it with your own voice, not with everyone else's.

01 / Listen

Hear what your voice is doing

A line follows your voice through the note bars as you sing, a glow shows whether you're in chest or head voice, and one plain word tells you how you sound right now: clear, airy, tight, nasal.

02 / Understand

Corrections that name the fix

When the same mistake keeps happening, a card appears with the physical change to make — "Open the back of your mouth, like the start of a yawn" — and the trainer plays you the wrong way, then the right way.

03 / Grow

Lessons built from your singing

Twelve step-by-step courses, warm-ups that check you're ready, and personal lessons built from the notes, jumps and vowels you miss most often.
