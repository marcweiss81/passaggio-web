Passaggio

Passaggio is an AI singing teacher on your Android phone. It listens as you sing and tells you how to improve, live. Everything runs on your phone: private, with no account.

Get the Android app How it works

Feel your technique

Hold a note. Six live guides read your sound: soft palate, throat space, free or pressed tone, breath, vowel shape and the bridge (where your voice shifts from chest to head). Each turns green when it's right.

Sing along to scrolling note bars and see your pitch live. When a habit keeps coming back, a card tells you what to change. After the lesson, Passaggio works out what went wrong and builds your next warm-up and lessons around it.

What Passaggio does

Most apps only check if you hit the note. Passaggio also hears how you make the sound (breath, strain, nasal tone, chest or head voice) and compares it with your own voice.

01 / While you sing

Live pitch and technique

Your voice draws a line through the note bars. One word tells you how you sound: clear, airy, tight or nasal.

02 / After each lesson

Ai Warm-up : finding your voice's path

It works out why notes went wrong (strain, the bridge, breath or a vowel), builds your next warm-up to fix it, then checks again. It's always the first warm-up option.

03 / Over time

Passaggio Ai Lessons

Lessons built from the notes you actually sing. The notes, jumps and vowels you keep missing get lessons of their own.
