The approach

Better singing starts with better understanding.

Most apps score pitch. Passaggio listens to how you make the sound — breath, pressing, nasality, strain, register — and judges it against your own voice, not a population average.

01 / Listen

Hear what your voice is doing

A live pitch line inside scrolling note bars, a chest-to-head resonance glow and a one-word tone read-out, all at 60 fps on the phone.

02 / Understand

Corrections that name the fix

When a habit repeats, a brass card appears with the body mechanic to change — "Lift the middle of your tongue" — and the trainer plays "not like this… like this".

03 / Grow

Lessons built from your singing

Twelve skill tracks, warm-ups with a ready check, and AI lessons generated from the notes, leaps, vowels and bridge crossings you miss most.
