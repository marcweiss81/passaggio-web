Needs Android 10 or newer and headphones (wired or Bluetooth). About 350 MB — the trainer's voice, sung examples and the sampled piano are bundled so nothing is streamed.

How to install

1

Tap Download latest APK on your phone (or copy the file across from a computer).

2

Open the file. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps, then come back and tap Install.

3

Plug in headphones, open Passaggio, and let the trainer walk you through voice setup. Allow the microphone when asked — it's the only permission the app needs.

Play Protect may warn that the app is from an unknown developer. That's expected for a sideloaded build; choose Install anyway. Updates install over the top and keep your data.

Verify the file

Every APK on the Releases page lists its SHA-256 digest. On a computer you can check it with sha256sum passaggio-candidate-*.apk (or certutil -hashfile … SHA256 on Windows) and compare it to the one shown beside the file.
