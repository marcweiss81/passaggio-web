Lessons

Twelve skill tracks, ten levels each.

  • Every lesson fits your range.
  • It starts by itself once you choose it.
  • Score 80% to open the next level.

Skill tracks

  1. 01

    Warm-up glides

    Gentle lip buzzes and hums that wake the voice up.

  2. 02

    Pitch accuracy

    Land each note right in the middle.

  3. 03

    Chord notes

    Skip between the notes of a chord.

  4. 04

    Breath and sustain

    Hold notes steady on one easy breath.

  5. 05

    Vowels

    Keep "ah", "ee" and "oo" clear on every note.

  6. 06

    Agility

    Move quickly and cleanly through scales.

  7. 07

    Short notes and starts

    Start notes cleanly, without sliding up into them.

  8. 08

    Leaps

    Jump between notes and land them cleanly.

  9. 09

    Loud and soft

    Swell a held note louder, then fade it away.

  10. 10

    Break and range

    Smooth the break between chest and head voice, and stretch your top.

  11. 11

    Mix voice

    Build one connected voice across and above your bridge.

  12. 12

    Ring and placement

    Find the forward buzz that carries without pushing.

More ways to practise

  1. 01
    After each lesson

    Ai Warm-up: finding your voice's path

    • Finds the likely reason notes went wrong: strain, the bridge, breath or a vowel.
    • Builds your next warm-up to fix it, then checks again.
    • Always the first warm-up option.
  2. 02
    Daily

    Today's plan

    One lesson a day, picked from your progress.

  3. 03
    Built from you

    Passaggio Ai Lessons

    • Lessons built from the notes you sing.
    • After about ten lessons, any note, jump, vowel or habit you keep missing gets its own lesson.
    • It retires after five clean runs.
  4. 04
    Before you sing

    Warm-ups

    Free the voice, Mix and Range, plus a Quick warm up. A check at the end tells you if you're ready to sing.

  5. 05
    Breath

    Breath and release

    Loosening moves, counted breathing and long, soft held notes.

  6. 06
    Style

    Judged in your style

    A belt, a breathy tone or a slide into a note is a mistake only if your style says so.
