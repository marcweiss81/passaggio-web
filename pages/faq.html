title: FAQ — Passaggio description: Answers about Passaggio: phones, headphones, privacy, scoring, singing styles, AI lessons and your own songs.

FAQ

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones. There is no iPhone version.

Do I need headphones?

Yes, whenever the piano or trainer plays, so the microphone hears only you. Wired or Bluetooth both work.

Does anything leave my phone?

No. There's no account, no cloud and no internet access. All analysis runs on the phone, and your results are stored encrypted.

Why did I score 79 when every note was in tune?

The app heard strain. Strain caps the score at 79, so you can't pass a level by pushing. Do a warm-up to free your voice, then try again.

It flagged my belt, but I sing rock.

Set your style before the exercise. Belt, breathy tone and twang are flagged only in styles that treat them as mistakes.

Why doesn't it flag every bad note?

It waits until it hears the same habit again, then shows one clear fix. Praise is kept for notes that are hard for you.

What does the Ai Warm-up do?

After each lesson it works out why notes went wrong (strain, the bridge, breath or a vowel), builds your next warm-up to fix it, then checks again.

What are Passaggio Ai Lessons?

Lessons built from the notes you sing. After about ten lessons, any note, jump, vowel, bridge crossing or habit you keep missing gets its own lesson in your range. It retires once you sing it clean five times in a row.

Can I sing my own songs?

Yes. Import a song from your phone. The app separates the vocals, finds the melody and syncs the lyrics, then you sing over the backing track.

Who is the trainer?

There are two trainer voices, female and male. The app picks the one that matches your voice; you can change it in Settings. Sung examples come from real singers.

Is it medical advice?

No. It's a practice tool and never diagnoses. If singing hurts or hoarseness lasts, see an ear, nose and throat doctor or a speech therapist.

Why isn't it on Google Play?

It's still in development. For now, download it from GitHub.
