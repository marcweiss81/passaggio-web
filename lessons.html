Lessons

Twelve tracks. Ten levels each.

Every lesson is fitted to your range, runs 60–150 seconds, and starts with the trainer singing the first note in your key. Score 80 % to open the next level; strain caps the score at 79 so the only way up is a free voice.

Daily

Today's plan

One lesson a day picked from your progress, with a warm-up chosen for it — the mix warm-up whenever the lesson crosses your bridge, "free the voice" whenever strain has been showing.

Before you sing

Warm-ups with a ready check

Free the voice, Mix warm-up, Range warm-up. Each exercise comes in a classical and a contemporary version (Garcia, Marchesi, Lamperti, Miller, Estill, CVT, SOVT…), names its strain risk, and ends with a 15-second check: three easy "ah" starts and one slow siren through the bridge.

Built from you

Passaggio AI lessons

After about ten lessons the app has enough evidence to establish a weak area — a specific note, a leap, a vowel, a bridge crossing or a habit — and generates a targeted lesson for it, inside your range. It retires once you've kept it clean five times in a row.

Breath

Breath and release

Loosening moves, counted breathing, a steady-hiss ladder, a long soft hold with your best times kept, and a placement check.

Style gate

Judged in your style

Declare classical, musical theatre, pop, rock, jazz or folk per exercise. A scoop isn't a miss in pop; belt, breath and twang are only flagged where the style treats them as faults.

Unlocks

80 % opens the next level

A weighted score from note accuracy and research-backed tone components. Nothing unlocks from pushing: strain caps the score at 79, silently.
