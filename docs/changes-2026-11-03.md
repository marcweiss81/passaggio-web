## Evidence and preventive checks

All eight required PNGs are in `docs/evidence/app-look-20261103/`: `before-index-360.png`, `before-index-1440.png`, `before-features-360.png`, `before-features-1440.png`, and the matching four `after-*` images. Before captures are served from an archive of `f4c5296`; after captures use this worktree. Viewports are exactly 360×3600 and 1440×2600.

The initial requested Chrome CLI invocation produced clipped mobile frames and some unloaded assets. `shoot.py` therefore uses the same installed headless Chrome through its local DevTools protocol, with only stdlib code, to set exact CSS viewport metrics and wait for images/fonts before capture. It has no Playwright dependency and adds no deployed JavaScript. Viewport/overflow, phone count/centring and hidden-screen request checks now fail the capture on regression.

Two selector conflicts were caught before delivery: the generic `.tile:not(.row)` lacquer selector also matched disclosures, and the original reduced-motion `.btn` selector lost to the primary button selector. Excluding disclosures and matching primary selector specificity fix their causes. Reviewed screenshots and Chrome computed-style checks are the prevention checks for future edits. Focus rings are inset on ivory buttons and summaries so they remain visible on the face rather than the black surround.

Tasks 2 and 3 remain outside this change: inner-page art, demo-canvas inks, merging the old CSS layers and WOFF2 conversion. Deployment and device work were not part of this task. No permission or evidence blocker remains.
