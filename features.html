Features

Everything here is in the current Android app. Open a section to see the details.

01

Feel your technique
  • Six live guides — soft palate, throat space, free or pressed sound, breath, vowel shape and the bridge. Each turns green when it's right.
  • 72-second guided practice — six steps, each with a vowel to sing and one thing to try.
  • Remember it — save the sound when it's right. Later lessons remind you of it.
  • First on the Technique tab. The guides compare sounds; they don't measure your throat.
02

Your voice
  • Voice setup — a short guided session finds your speaking pitch, range, voice type and bridge, and records your own vowels. Your tone is then judged against you.
  • Find your bridge — your voice map: your range on a keyboard, your most comfortable notes and your bridge. A live figure shows whether you're singing in chest or head voice.
  • Range growth — tracked from your first setup.
  • Six styles — classical, musical theatre, pop, rock, jazz and folk. Corrections follow your style.
03

While you sing
  • Note lane — note bars scroll past and your voice draws a line through them. Gold means on pitch.
  • Guide piano — plays each note just before you sing it.
  • Technique, not just notes — it hears breath, strain, pressing, nasal tone, twang, belt, vibrato and whether you're in chest or head voice, judged against your own voice.
  • Tone word — one word for how you sound: clear, airy, tight, nasal or creaky.
  • Corrections — when a habit comes back, a card shows the fix. Tap it to hear the difference.
  • Streaks — sing notes in tune in a row and the bars catch fire.
  • Scoring — 80% opens the next level. Strain caps the score at 79, so you can't pass by pushing.
  • Glossary — tap any singing term for a plain explanation.
  • Results — round by round, with what to work on next.
04

Lessons
  • Ai Warm-up: finding your voice's path — after each lesson it works out why notes went wrong (strain, the bridge, breath or a vowel), builds your next warm-up to fix it, then checks again. Always the first warm-up option.
  • Passaggio Ai Lessons — lessons built from the notes you actually sing.
  • Isolated weak area targeting — after about ten lessons, any note, jump, vowel, bridge crossing or habit you keep missing gets its own lesson. It retires after five clean runs.
  • Twelve skill tracks, ten levels each — from warm-up glides to mix voice. See all tracks →
  • Today's plan — one lesson a day, picked from your progress.
  • Warm-ups — Free the voice, Mix and Range, with a check that you're ready to sing. Also breath exercises and a Quick warm up.
  • Lessons start by themselves once you choose one. Favourites keep the ones you like close.
05

Your songs
  • Import from your phone — the vocals are separated, the melody found and the lyrics synced, all on the phone.
  • Lyrics in the note bars — each word sits in its bar, with a karaoke-style teleprompter.
  • Chords — the current and next chord, worked out from the song.
  • Song ideas — songs that suit your voice type, by genre. Tap "Not for me" to see fewer like it.
06

Trainer
  • Two voices — a female and a male trainer. Matched to your voice; change it in Settings.
  • Real sung examples — a real singer sings the first note in your key.
  • Hear the difference — for common mistakes you hear "Not like this", then "Like this", then the advice.
  • Voice commands — say "next", "again", "start" or "skip".
07

Setup
  • Headphones — wired or Bluetooth. A quick check measures their delay so the bars match what you hear.
  • Volume meter — shows if you're singing at a good level.
  • Save my singing — optional. Recordings stay on your phone.
08

Appearance
  • Choose colour schemes, fonts, button styles and where controls sit.
  • Line skins for your voice line unlock over your first 20 lessons.
